  3. Property owner authorizes Prairie Ridge Exteriors, its representatives, their affiliates, and or its attorney(s) to access the property as needed, execute work authorizations on property owner’s behalf, furnish materials, supply equipment, and perform labor necessary to complete the claim related repairs within the scope of loss provided by the carrier for the insurance proceeds.

Property owners agree that any portion of work, deductible, betterment, non recoverable depreciation, non insured code upgrades or additional work requested but not covered by the carrier is ultimately their responsibility above the carrier proceeds.
  5. Property owner hereby directs insurance and or mortgage company, including their claims representatives, to release any and all information or documents requested by Prairie Ridge Exteriors including but not limited to the scheduling of inspections, estimates for repairs, payments or drafts and or financial arrangements of any kind regarding this claim on property owner’s behalf for the purpose of obtaining actual benefits to be paid by the carrier for services rendered or to be rendered.

Property owner hereby assigns any and all insurance rights, post loss benefits, proceeds and causes of action under any applicable insurance policies to Prairie Ridge Exteriors and authorizes and directs insurance and or mortgage company to name “Prairie Ridge Exteriors” as an additional payee on any and all payments for repairs. In the event property owner fails to fulfill their obligations or otherwise terminates this agreement after any applicable statutory waiting period for any reason other than the denial of coverage by the carrier, property owner agrees to pay Prairie Ridge Exteriors twenty percent of the current “Replacement Cost Value” specified by the carrier, not as a penalty, but rather as payment for services rendered.
